# Authoring and editorial desk

Use the host's actual writing ability; no separate model service is mandatory. Produce the requested manuscript, not merely advice about writing one. Persist it with the commands in `12-complete-workflow.md`.

## Commission and draft

Develop a promise the author's knowledge, sources, time and budget can support. Record buyer motivation separately. Translate observed competitor gaps into specific design choices and reader-testing hypotheses. Create chapter contracts with a stable ID, title, purpose and concrete deliverable. Practical nonfiction should move from the reader's starting point to usable outcomes; fiction uses scene function and causality.

Prepare a voice sample grounded in genuine author material or chosen characteristics. Match specificity, vocabulary and rhythm without borrowing someone else's identity or life story. Get a meaningful decision on the promise, plan and sample before extensive production. Save the actual author declaration; do not manufacture approval.

For each chapter, retrieve its contract, relevant sources, accepted terms/continuity and necessary transitions. Draft using the available host model, then save the exact body, provenance, rights basis and change summary. Label hypothetical examples. Never invent interviews, credentials, studies, quotations or personal stories and attribute them to the user.

Review whether the chapter fulfills its job: Are the steps usable? Are terms introduced before use? What does it add? Which sentences can be removed without losing meaning? Replace abstractions with relevant detail or delete them. Do not add motivational filler to reach a word count.

## Evidence and targeted revision

Use the executable [editorial handoff](25-editorial-handoff.md) to retrieve full current text and record version-bound checks. Its coverage makes selected-chapter work explicit; an outline, earlier sample or expansion summary is not the finished manuscript. Use smaller batches when necessary, then check the saved coverage before describing a whole-book pass.

For material factual/experience claims, quote the exact sentence in the current chapter, link stored source IDs and record a supported/pending/contradicted finding with reasoning. Helpers check linkage and staleness, not semantic truth or source authenticity. A blank ledger does not prove there are no unsupported claims. Specialist health/legal/financial work needs qualified review.

Check repeated explanations, incompatible definitions and contradictions across chapters. Keep a continuity object for facts established, promises made, unresolved questions and dependencies. This is a working record, not an automatic contradiction-proof engine.

Revise the affected passage, explain the improvement and preserve earlier drafts. Do not silently rewrite approved sections or change the reader promise to fit a weak draft. Recheck downstream artifacts after substantive changes.

## Review gates

- Model critique is labelled reviewer_type=model and independent=false.
- Editorial evaluation records actual human feedback on the exact manuscript.
- Reader evaluation records real target-reader findings, including confusion and disagreements.
- Author approval is separate from editorial review and external publication permission.

The helper does not authenticate reviewer identities. Never create fictitious human reviews to clear a gate. If no reviewer participated, produce the review copy and brief while leaving the gate open.

Ask readers what they expected, where they lost the thread, which task they could complete, what remained unanswered and what seemed unnecessary. For fiction, ask about engagement, clarity, causality, character motivation and the ending. Avoid leading questions. Obtain consent before quoting feedback publicly. Private feedback is separate from a customer review; contacting anyone requires authorization.
