# Book production and editing

Accept existing drafts, notes, interviews and voice transcripts. Do not force a finished author through niche research unless requested. Preserve originals.

Before extensive drafting, approve a brief, a genre-appropriate outline and a voice sample. Maintain project memory with approved facts, style, terminology, chapter ownership, sources, character/world references and unresolved decisions. Retrieve the relevant parts per chapter rather than replaying the whole chat.

Nonfiction: identify what each chapter adds; research material claims; distinguish fact, advice, opinion and illustrative examples. Do not use automatically invented personal stories or citations. Give practical examples and avoid unsupported claims of expertise. Specialist health/legal/financial material needs appropriately qualified review, not just a generic disclaimer.

Fiction: maintain timeline, character motives, relationships, locations, viewpoint, tense, setup/payoff and unresolved plot threads. Give scenes distinct functions. A fiction manuscript does not need references for invented events, but factual claims presented as real need appropriate scrutiny.

Children: distinguish picture books, early readers, middle-grade/chapter books and young-adult fiction. Verify target reading age with human review. Plan page/spread or scene composition before expensive art. Keep characters and visual references consistent. Do not treat an automated reading score as a full age-suitability check.

Comics: track panels, action, reading order, lettering and speech bubbles separately from art. Activity books: solve generated puzzles programmatically; check duplicates, answer-key agreement and printable usability. Low-content products need their own channel eligibility checks.

Draft limited sections against their approved purpose. Review for substance, unsupported claims, repetitions, contradictions, voice, accessibility and structure. A reviewer proposes localized edits; it does not silently rewrite approved chapters. Save revisions, evidence and a change summary. User approval of revised text is recorded without claiming it changes the platform definition of AI-generated content.

Originality means a distinct contribution and defensible sourcing. Similarity screening has incomplete coverage; never certify universal uniqueness or plagiarism-free status. Human voice means authentic expression, not evasion of AI detectors.
