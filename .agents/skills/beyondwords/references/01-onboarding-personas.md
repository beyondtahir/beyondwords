# Intake, reader, buyer and author voice

Retrieve saved project context first. Determine country, legal publishing route, book language, reader age where applicable, genre, author knowledge, available source material, desired formats, budget and currency, and any exclusivity commitments. Do not collect identity documents or bank details into the manuscript workspace. A pen name is not a legal account identity.

Produce three separate profiles:
- Reader: starting knowledge, goals or desired story experience, vocabulary, needs, accessibility and age considerations.
- Buyer: purchase context, objections, expectations and evidence needed. Mark hypotheses as hypotheses; do not invent interviews.
- Author voice: user-owned samples and transcripts, genuine experiences, tone, rhythm, level of detail, humour boundaries and disliked phrasing. No invented qualifications or experiences.

Approve an inexpensive sample before producing a full manuscript or illustration set. For Urdu/Roman Urdu input, preserve meaning and let the user review important translation choices. Interface language does not establish platform book-language eligibility.

Book brief: reader, promise, original contribution, genre, scope, outline, evidence needs, illustration plan, channels, estimated costs and open decisions. Page counts are provisional until layout; do not impose a universal eight-chapter or 100-page book.

Use `assets/book-brief.md` and `assets/voice-profile.md`. Initialize a fresh local project with the helper only after the user selects a project folder.
