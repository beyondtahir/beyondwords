# Local persistence and compatibility

Use the unified `scripts/beyondwords.py` workflow and reference 12 for new books. Existing compatibility projects remain readable through `scripts/publishing_core.py project`. These local store operations do not log in, browse, generate prose, publish or spend.

Store mutations require the exact current revision. Use read/history to inspect actual state after an interruption or conflict before retrying. Decisions and artifacts retain revisions and hashes; changed content invalidates affected review status. An actor declaration is not authenticated human approval.

Legacy migration preserves original files and records and rejects unknown schemas. Keep author workspaces outside the installation and distribution. Do not mix synthetic evidence with real project observations. The schema remains in assets/project-state-v2.schema.json for compatibility.

Current research and production capabilities live in the unified toolkit. Compatibility-state labels do not override its permission, review or account controls.
