# Launch and business desk

Create listing copy, sample, author bio, cover previews, outreach drafts and a dated calendar from the actual book. Save `launch` with budget, channels, success measures and stop conditions. Prepare the work yourself; ask only for missing facts or meaningful choices. Sending messages or spending money needs explicit authorization.

Research relevant communities and free reader routes afresh, including participation rules. Choose channels suited to audience access, time and budget. Do not prescribe generic daily posting or call an unverified service free. Record experiments, costs and results.

## Reviews

Private reader feedback improves the book; public reviews are voluntary opinions. Never write as a customer, fabricate experience, arrange swaps, reward positive reviews or condition a free copy on reviewing.

Adapt a neutral request: “If you choose to read it, I'd appreciate your honest feedback. A public review is optional; there is no obligation and no expectation of a positive review.” This is a draft, not permission to contact anyone.

[KDP customer-review guidance](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202101910), retrieved 30 September 2026, permits free/discounted books without requiring or influencing reviews. Recheck current rules before outreach.

## Ads and reports

Prepare targeting hypotheses from permitted research and an affordable experiment. Do not impose a universal review count, bid or daily budget. Separate experiments so results can be interpreted.

Campaign creation/change/pause needs actual account access, marketplace/ad eligibility, exact settings, budget/bid bounds and spending approval. No live ad adapter is bundled. [Amazon Ads API access](https://advertising.amazon.com/about-api) has an actual access process. Never claim monitoring or pausing without a real result. Recurring monitoring additionally needs a real scheduler and authorized data connection or import cadence.

Use `report-import` now. Inspect real CSV headers and map them explicitly. Preserve source, period, account label, marketplace, title ID, currency, estimated/finalized status and attribution window. Reconcile to the supplied source total. Duplicate/overlapping reports need reconciliation, not double counting.

Interpret impressions, clicks, spend and attributed orders with reporting lag and sample size. Ad-attributed retail sales are not royalties, profit or proof of incremental impact. Explain the observation, competing explanations, proposed test and stop rule before recommending a change. The analyzer does arithmetic; it never changes campaigns.

## Income planning

Clarify monthly/cumulative, royalties/profit/cash, currency and tax treatment. `goals` supports monthly pre-tax profit with actual inputs or explicitly labelled assumptions. Printing/delivery already deducted from royalties must not be deducted twice.

For positive contribution, required paid sales = ceiling((target + monthly fixed costs) / contribution per sale). This establishes no demand. Catalog size remains unknown until actual per-title results justify scenarios. Production time uses hours per stage and available weekly hours; external delays are separate. No guaranteed income date or BSR-based earnings.

`finance` compares matched-period royalties, ad spend and separately incurred costs. Missing reports remain unknown, never zero. Keep currencies separate without a documented conversion. Estimated royalties remain estimates; payouts/taxes/cash timing need their own records.
