# Eligibility, rights and publishing preparation

Consult current official guidance for the selected platform, country, language, edition, payments, identity, taxes, AI disclosure, metadata, ISBN use, content restrictions and exclusivity. Keep checked_at, source, rule version and scope. Before a consequential action, refresh the relevant rules; on failure, do not present stale guidance as current approval.

Review AI-generated text, translations, cover images and interior art separately. Editing generated material does not by itself change Amazon's disclosed AI-generated category. Record source provenance; do not optimize for hiding AI use.

Rights checks flag unresolved permissions, copyright/trademark/publicity issues and licensing limitations. They are not comprehensive legal clearance or a global trademark search. Distinguish correctable file issues, title rejection, review holds and account issues. Do not promise account safety or sales.

KDP, Lulu ebook distribution, Lulu bookstore sales and Lulu Print API fulfillment are separate adapters/workflows. Do not assume an undocumented upload API. Guided publication is a valid deliverable. Recheck exact retailer capabilities before claiming integration. A print order being accepted is not proof of ebook retail distribution.

Prepare accurate metadata, identifiers, descriptions, categories and keywords without misleading stuffing. Never invent an ISBN. Check channel conflict/exclusivity before multi-channel distribution. Never mislabel language or identity to get through a form.

Use the account owner's attended login. They personally complete MFA/identity/banking/tax declarations. Before sharing files or final publication, show the exact channel, account, title, files/checksums, price, territories and disclosures for confirmation.

Publication states: PREPARED → USER_APPROVED → SUBMITTED → UNDER_REVIEW → APPROVED → LISTING_VERIFIED. A successful upload is not approval. After uncertain submission, reconcile the dashboard/receipt before retrying. Do not auto-create a new account or resubmit rejected material through a different identity.

Use preflight reports for the exact artifact version. The starter aggregator only checks supplied report structure and completeness; it does not execute the individual reviews or grant authorization. The production connector must enforce approval separately.
