# KDP preparation — guidance, not an account integration

Use current official guidance and the project's policy evidence tracker. No upload automation or account access is implemented. The account owner handles identity, banking, tax and final submission directly. Do not assume country eligibility, review time, payout schedule, title-upload limits or account safety.

Prepare accurate title/subtitle, contributors, description, appropriate categories/keywords, rights territories, format-specific files and a pricing scenario. Do not invent a prohibition on all dates or all brand mentions; review each usage and current policy in context. Metadata must describe the actual book without misleading promotional claims.

KDP's [Content Guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390) distinguish AI-generated text, images and translations from AI-assisted work. Record provenance honestly and review the current disclosure questions, including substantially edited generated content. The local audit cannot guarantee visibility, approval or account outcomes.

Review [eBook price requirements](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200634560) for the selected marketplace, file size, royalty option and other eligibility conditions. The inherited royalty helper is a limited US scenario calculator, not a live eligibility service. Publication, account actions and marketing execution are later milestones.
