# Review boundaries

Do not score sellability, predict bans or promise account safety. Check evidence freshness, exact edition, rights records, genuine author contributions, metadata accuracy and AI-generated text/images/translations. Current KDP/Lulu policy evidence requires human review before changing publishing checks. Heuristic flags require investigation; a green metadata result is not a release approval.

Independent editorial review, actual-reader evaluation and channel-specific technical validation remain release gates. Record exact reviewed artifact hashes and invalidate reviews when artifacts or relevant policies change.
