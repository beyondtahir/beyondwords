# Evidence-led niche research

Use the consolidated research workflow. Record actual reader/buyer needs, author experience and budget. Research permitted candidate sources afresh for each project. Select edition/ASIN, marketplace, format, capture time, currency, overall versus category rank and paid/free list. Missing fields remain UNKNOWN; snapshots and review counts cannot establish sales or profitability.

Inspect rights-cleared samples and covers for reader expectations and gaps; record interpretations separately from facts. Validate an original promise with real readers. A niche remains a hypothesis until independent evidence supports it. There is no universal rank, review-count or price-band threshold that guarantees success.
