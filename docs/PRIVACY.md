# Your work stays separate

Keep author memory, manuscripts, account state and reports in a private workspace outside this package. The source and skill downloads contain no author's project, test-run output, account session or conversation transcript.

Use your assistant's actual permission controls. Enter passwords, verification codes, identity documents, bank details and tax declarations directly into the official service. Do not paste them into book notes or commit them to a repository.

Shared storage or cross-host memory needs explicit access and a verified handoff. Installing Beyondwords does not expose unrelated chats or synchronize accounts. Publishing, paid actions and monitoring use the scope you authorize and the tools actually available.

For vulnerabilities or suspected credential exposure, follow the [security policy](../SECURITY.md) and use its private reporting route. Public issues are for non-sensitive support requests.
