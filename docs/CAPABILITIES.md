# Your publishing team, step by step

[← Back to Beyondwords](../README.md) · [Install](INSTALL.md) · [Technical guide](TECHNICAL.md)

Beyondwords is a goal-driven book production house for building a publishing catalogue or delivering book-production services, with Amazon Kindle/KDP as its primary platform focus. It combines a conversational workflow with executable local tools. It can perform available work, keep the project organized and carry decisions forward. The host supplies reasoning, writing, images and browsing; connected actions additionally need real access and authorization.

## Two business uses, one production workflow

**Own-book publishing:** work from an income goal through reader/niche research, creation, publication and marketing. Use actual royalties and costs to assess progress and inform further investment. KDP is the primary focus; Lulu preparation and supported attended routes are also included.

**Client production services:** use the same research, writing, editing, cover and file tools to produce agreed work for Upwork or direct clients. Keep projects and approvals separate, validate deliverables and agree rights, AI use, scope and revisions with the client. This is a use of the existing toolkit, not a bundled freelance marketplace integration, client-acquisition system or guaranteed source of paid work.

An income target guides planning. The toolkit cannot establish demand, future profit or the number of clients/books required without suitable evidence. The goal/report features below concern the book business; they do not provide client invoicing or Upwork earnings synchronization.

## 1. Start with the person

The first conversation establishes country, language, publishing experience, interests, reader, intended formats, time, budget and goals. Returning authors confirm saved information and resolve conflicts. Different authors and books remain separate.

Choose a guided collaboration or a more delegated workflow within an agreed scope. The assistant should do useful work between decisions and keep responses brief. It should not turn an income goal into a long unsolicited document or silently decide your budget.

**Outputs:** saved intake, selected project, confirmed decisions and the next useful question or action.

## 2. Research readers and original opportunities

Research starts with a specific reader and marketplace. It compares multiple books, inspects permitted samples and actual reader feedback, then checks whether the proposed gap survives counterexamples. Listing claims, preview observations, reviews and creative interpretations remain distinct.

Without a selected niche, the intended comparison is five researched niches and one concrete book idea in each. With a selected niche, it is five distinct ideas within it. Insufficient evidence means more research or an explicit incomplete result, not five invented recommendations.

The tools record source URL, capture time, selected edition/ASIN, marketplace, format, price/currency and scoped ranks when available. Overall/category and paid/free ranks are kept separate. The default route is the host's normal browser; a read-only Playwright adapter and authorized evidence imports provide additional routes.

**Outputs:** a short source-linked comparison, candidate reader gaps, contrary evidence, unknowns, feasibility questions and a small prototype to test.

**Boundary:** there is no exact search-volume feed or calibrated rank-to-sales estimator. A rank observation does not establish royalties, profit, conversion or future demand. Source access and reuse permissions remain separate from technical reachability.

## 3. Shape the voice and the book

Develop an original persona for this book: audience, vocabulary, rhythm, viewpoint, tone, examples and things to avoid. Ask for the author's actual experience where relevant; do not fabricate it. Approve a sample before expanding into a full draft.

Fiction uses character motives, world rules, causality, tension and payoff. Nonfiction uses reader outcomes, chapter responsibilities and sourced claims. Visual books start with an art direction and page prototype. A specific, natural voice is an editorial objective, not a claim that AI-generated work has human authorship.

Local tools support versioned plans, chapter contracts, writing context, supplied manuscript import, chapter history and story memory. They help keep changes and unresolved threads visible while the host drafts and revises.

**Outputs:** creative brief, persona, approved sample, outline, chapters, story bible and revision history.

## 4. Review the actual manuscript

Prepare full-text review packets and connect claims to sources. Track chapter coverage, structure, consistency, repetition and reader promise. A review must refer to the current manuscript; edits can make earlier approvals stale.

The assistant can critique and revise, organize human feedback and prepare an editorial handoff. Independent editorial judgment and real-reader evaluation remain separate release gates. A second model review is not a real-reader test.

**Outputs:** review packets, claim/source ledger, issue list, revision decisions and a clearly scoped editorial handoff.

## 5. Create artwork and covers

Study relevant cover conventions, genre fit and small-size legibility. Develop original concepts, generate artwork through an available host tool or use supplied authorized assets, then compose editable typography and inspect the result.

Plan native pixel dimensions against the intended physical size. The preferred working target is 310 effective DPI where applicable; the selected retailer's current specification governs delivery. Enlarging an undersized file or changing its DPI metadata does not add original detail. Final print wraps depend on actual trim, page count, paper and the current retailer template.

The toolkit includes cover directions, composition, dimension/pixel checks, wrap assembly and visual contact sheets. Optional design-tool editing depends on a real connection or a manual handoff.

**Outputs:** original cover directions, editable composition resources, front cover or full wrap, preview images and technical checks.

## 6. Produce the interior

| Route | Available output | Review still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Text-led book | Reflowable EPUB, print PDF and basic editable DOCX | Typography, navigation, page breaks and retailer preview |
| Illustrated, children's or cartoon-style book | Host-assisted text/art direction, image-page PDF and fixed-layout EPUB | Age fit, art consistency, reading order, device behavior and accessibility |
| Comics | Panel/dialogue planning and assembly of supplied or host-created pages | Lettering, sequence, specialist layout and device navigation |
| Coloring book | Page assembly, optional blank backs, PDF and contact sheet | Line quality, originality, bleed and physical usability |
| Puzzle book | Supported text/image assembly and editorial workflow | Puzzle solvability, uniqueness, answer keys and suitable layout |

The tools do not constitute a universal puzzle generator or a complete desktop publishing engine. Complex diagrams, mixed layouts, right-to-left typography and every retailer/device combination require additional verification. Use a physical proof for print decisions.

**Outputs:** production files, editable source where supported, artifact hashes, contact sheets and validation findings. EPUBCheck is an optional independent validator, installed separately.

### Audiobook route

The bundled audiobook material is **guidance only**. It helps organize a production handoff and review the current requirements for a chosen narration/distribution route. There is no native narration engine, audio mastering pipeline, measured audio-quality validator or audiobook account uploader. Those steps need separately available tools or providers and their own eligibility, rights and technical checks. Do not sell or describe an unproduced audio file as a completed deliverable.

## 7. Prepare identity, metadata and publication

Use current official guidance for the author's real country, identity, tax, payment and account requirements. Develop an original public author/publisher presentation informed by relevant examples. Sensitive account information is entered by the owner directly into the service.

Title, subtitle, description, categories and keywords should fit the actual book and observed search behavior. Suggestions and relevant results provide signals, not exact search counts or a promise of viral reach. Cover and listing claims must remain consistent with the contents.

The toolkit prepares exact-file packages and supports scoped, attended publishing workflows for KDP and additional marketplace routes where the necessary controls are available. It binds actions to selected files and records uncertain outcomes for reconciliation instead of blindly resubmitting.

**Outputs:** account/setup checklist, public profile copy, metadata, file package, preview findings and submission status where actually observed.

**Boundary:** KDP has no invented public upload API here. Account login, rights declarations, AI-content disclosures, preview decisions and final action scope require the real owner and available controls. Submission, retailer approval and a live listing are different states. Additional platform routes are not universal account certification.

## 8. Launch and reach readers

Plan relevant outreach, advance-reader feedback and optional unbiased reviews. There are no paid customer-review schemes, review swaps or positive-only review requests.

For Amazon Ads, the implemented Sponsored Products adapter supports scoped API actions, campaign planning/assembly, recorded requests and recovery. Actual use requires approved account/API access and credentials. Agree on objectives, budget, spend limits and permitted changes before execution. A connected host tool can provide a separate authorized route when available.

Report imports offer a working analysis route without API access. Interpret search terms, targets, placements and campaigns within the fields the reports actually contain. Do not invent demographic targeting or missing attribution.

**Outputs:** launch actions, bounded experiments, campaign drafts, recorded authorized actions and report-based recommendations.

## 9. Understand the business and continue

Separate ad-attributed retail sales from author royalties and net profit. Consider printing/delivery, production, advertising, returns, fees, timing and any relevant owner-supplied costs. An earnings goal becomes a scenario with explicit inputs and uncertainty; it is not a prediction inferred from bestseller rank.

A local monitoring worker and scheduler handoff records support continuing report review. They need a running environment, configured data access and an actual schedule. Installing a skill does not create an always-on service or automatically grant future spending authority.

**Outputs:** scoped financial scenarios, imported report analysis, experiment records, monitor receipts and next actions.

## Capability boundaries at a glance

| Capability | Included | Required separately |
|---|---|---|
| Guided workflow and private memory | Skill + local store | Accessible persistent workspace |
| Research and evidence handling | Import, browser adapter, edition/freshness checks | Permitted source access and actual observations |
| Drafting and creative collaboration | Personas, plans, context and revision tools | Host inference and author direction |
| Artwork creation | Specifications and composition workflow | Host image tool or supplied original assets |
| EPUB/PDF/DOCX production | Supported local exporters | Optional libraries and format-specific review |
| Publishing and ads actions | Implemented scoped adapters | Account access, current eligibility and authorization |
| Monitoring | Local worker and handoff records | Running worker/scheduler and report access |
| Local inference | Replaceable AI interface | Tested local-model path remains a future requirement |
